# Video 07 — Surplus, Shortage & Equilibrium

**Market:** Cheese (pounds per week)
**Equations:** Qd = 100 - 2P, Qs = 3P - 50
**Equilibrium:** P* = 30, Q* = 40
**Example prices:** P = 40 (surplus of 50), P = 25 (shortage of 25)
