# Video 01 — Scarcity, Opportunity Cost & the PPF

**Scenario:** Island economy — Coconuts (C) vs. Fish (F)
**Simple PPF (constant opportunity cost):** F = 100 - 2C, intercepts (0, 100) and (50, 0)
**Realistic PPF (bowed-out, increasing opportunity cost):** F = 100·sqrt(1 - (C/50)²), same intercepts
**Points on the bowed curve:** A = (30, 80), B = (40, 60), C = (50, 0)
**Opportunity cost:** A→B costs 2 fish per coconut; B→C costs 6 fish per coconut (increasing)
**Off-curve points:** D = (20, 40) inefficient (inside), E = (40, 90) unattainable (outside)
**Growth scenario:** new frontier F = 130·sqrt(1 - (C/65)²) — resources/technology up ~30%, E becomes attainable
